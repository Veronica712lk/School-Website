About Us

Our school, established in 1993, is a renowned educational institution located in Lusaka, Zambia. We are dedicated to providing high-quality education and nurturing the holistic development of young women in a supportive and inspiring environment.

History

Founded with the vision to empower young girls through education, our school has grown significantly over the years. Our school has been at the forefront of educational excellence, offering a comprehensive curriculum that includes academic, artistic, and extracurricular activities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to foster a learning environment that promotes intellectual curiosity, personal growth, and ethical values. We aim to prepare our students to become confident, responsible, and successful individuals who contribute positively to society.

Core Values

  • Academic Excellence
  • Personal Integrity
  • Community Engagement
  • Respect and Inclusivity
  • Leadership Development

Achievements

Our school has received numerous accolades for academic excellence and community service. Our students consistently perform well in national examinations and are recognized for their contributions to various social initiatives.

Community Involvement

We believe in the importance of giving back to the community. Our school regularly organizes outreach programs, environmental conservation projects, and charitable activities, fostering a spirit of service among our students.
