About the Annual Agricultural Show

The Agricultural and Commercial Show of Zambia is a major event that showcases the country's achievements in agriculture, commerce, and industry. It brings together participants from diverse sectors to celebrate innovation, creativity, and the spirit of competition. Our students proudly represented our school, showcasing their talents in various categories and bringing home victories in art, poetry, and essay writing.

Art Competition

Our talented artists won top positions with their vibrant and meaningful artwork that highlighted environmental conservation and cultural heritage.

Poetry

The poetry entries captivated the judges with their creativity and depth, earning our students recognition for their powerful words and expressions.

Essay Writing

Our essayists impressed with their insightful writings on the theme of sustainability and agricultural innovation, securing wins in multiple categories.
