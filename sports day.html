About the Event

The Interhouse Sports Competition is one of the most anticipated events of the year at Mary Queen of Peace Girls' School. It's a time when students from different houses compete in a range of sports showcasing their skills, sportsmanship, and team spirit. This year was filled with unforgettable moments and outstanding achievements.
