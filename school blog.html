This story was written in response to the 2021 English Internal Exam Paper, where the question required starting a story with: "Only after she died did people know who she really was..."

Short Story

Only after she died did people know who she really was. As the magnificent Warren Buffet once quoted, "It takes twenty years to build a reputation and five minutes to ruin it." This was especially true for Sharisha Sinduluma, who eerily died at the age of twenty, echoing the sentiment of Warren's quote. Sharishas calm benign smile, accentuated by her buoyant personality, made people to think she was a reserved ingenuous woman, which, as discovered at her time of death, was far from true.

Hardly had the sun stopped shining on her when the police realized that this woman, who appeared to be vibrant as elaborated in her elegy, was actually a cold serial killer. Her visits to her hometown were as infrequent as those of a perpetual school dodger attending classes. Everyone knew she was out on business trips, but in actuality, she was in Thailand killing innocent children for monetary gains. Detective Bwalya, known for his acumen in judgment, stumbled upon her case and dealt with it for a fortnight. Thereafter, he concluded that Sharisha Sinduluma was indeed a serial killer.

The revelations obtained from her case were indeed a shock to her family and friends. Sharisha's mother, who had believed her daughter's lies for many years, said to the press in an interview, "Only after she died did people know who she really was. Our own Sharisha deluded us!"
